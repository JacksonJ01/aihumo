# AIHuMo application guides

Purpose and operating steps for every application in the Main Hub.

## AIHuMo Pipeline

Process recordings, build pose features, label movement, align cameras, and inspect synchronized measurements.

### Before you start

- Choose a recording or an existing AIHuMo workspace.
- Confirm the participant, session, and camera details.

### How to use

1. Open the workflow you need from Data Studio.
2. Process raw video in Mode 0, or open existing processed data.
3. Annotate, align, or review graphs, then save before leaving the workspace.

### Review

- Check warnings and missing-pose sections.
- Confirm saved files belong to the intended session and recording.

## Justice Dance

Practice rhythm, coordination, and movement consistency with music-led prompts.

### Before you start

- Clear enough space to move safely.
- Place the camera where your full body remains visible.

### How to use

1. Choose the routine and difficulty.
2. Complete the short movement-range check.
3. Follow the timing and pose cues, then finish the round.

### Review

- Review timing accuracy, movement consistency, and replay similarity.

### Safety and notes

Stop if the space becomes obstructed or movement feels unsafe.

## Flow Zone

React to target, escape, and avoidance zones while building agility and spatial control.

### Before you start

- Clear the floor and keep the full play area in frame.
- Complete calibration so zones match your safe reach.

### How to use

1. Choose the grid size and difficulty.
2. Enter, leave, or avoid the highlighted zone as instructed.
3. Return to a ready position between prompts.

### Review

- Review reaction time, accuracy, overshoot, and time in the correct zone.

### Safety and notes

Use a difficulty that stays inside your comfortable movement range.

## Mirror Match

Copy a reference pose or movement and improve symmetry, stability, and synchronization.

### Before you start

- Select a reference recording, live mirror, or partner mode.
- Stand where the camera can see the required joints.

### How to use

1. Complete calibration.
2. Match the displayed pose or movement.
3. Hold steady until the prompt completes.

### Review

- Review joint similarity, left-right symmetry, hold stability, and timing.

## Balance Challenge

Practice controlled weight shifts and stable holds within a calibrated safe range.

### Before you start

- Use a level, non-slip surface.
- Keep a stable support nearby if you may need it.

### How to use

1. Calibrate your comfortable standing and reaching range.
2. Follow the center-control or freeze-pose target.
3. Return to a stable neutral position after each prompt.

### Review

- Review sway, hold time, target accuracy, and recovery control.

### Safety and notes

Stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, or unsteadiness.

## Sports Reaction

Train sport-style reaction, reach, recovery, and directional control.

### Before you start

- Clear the play area and choose a drill appropriate for the space.
- Complete the ready-stance calibration.

### How to use

1. Start from the ready position.
2. Respond to each directional or reach prompt.
3. Recover to a controlled stance before the next prompt.

### Review

- Review reaction time, reach accuracy, recovery time, and stance consistency.

### Safety and notes

Avoid drills that require more room or speed than the environment safely permits.

## Rehabilitation Lab

Review controlled range-of-motion and stability tasks using clinician-defined targets.

### Before you start

- Use only tasks and limits approved for the participant.
- Position the camera to keep the measured area visible.

### How to use

1. Select the assigned task and tolerance.
2. Complete calibration without forcing the movement.
3. Perform each repetition slowly and stop the session if needed.

### Review

- Review range, control, stability, and changes between sessions.

### Safety and notes

This is an analysis aid, not medical advice. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness, or unusual symptoms.

## Ergonomics Lab

Review posture, lifting, reaching, bending, and repetitive movement patterns.

### Before you start

- Choose a camera view that clearly shows the task.
- Keep the normal work area and equipment in frame.

### How to use

1. Record or load the task.
2. Perform the movement naturally.
3. Review flagged positions, then repeat after making an adjustment.

### Review

- Compare posture duration, repetition, reach, bending, and movement consistency.

### Safety and notes

Use the results as a review aid; qualified workplace or clinical guidance takes priority.

## Gait and Locomotion

### Review
